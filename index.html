AlreadyGotIt

A personal purchase-history tool that helps its operator avoid buying duplicate products.

What it does

AlreadyGotIt reads purchase-confirmation emails from supported retailers, extracts product and order details, and builds a private ownership catalog for its operator.

  • Requests read-only Gmail access.
  • Searches only for supported retailer purchase emails.
  • Processes and stores data on the operator's private Atlas server.
  • Does not provide public registration or public access to the catalog.

Google user data

Google user data is used only to identify and organize the operator's own purchases. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

AlreadyGotIt's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
